Victor Von Doom

Monarch // Sorcerer // Scientist
"There is no magic. There is no science. There is only Doom."

Born to a witch and a healer in the Romani camps of Latveria, I was forged in the fires of loss and vengeance. I do not seek power for power's sake; I seek it because no other hand is steady enough to hold the world.

Temporal Database

1999
The Empire State Incident
Attempted to construct a trans-dimensional communicator to rescue my mother's soul from Mephisto. The machine failed due to a miscalculation by Richards. The resulting explosion scarred my face and opened my eyes.
2003
The Tibetan Forge
Traveled the Himalayas. Mastered the ways of the monks and forged my first suit of nuclear-powered armor. The mask was applied while still hot—a permanent reminder of my resolve.
2005
The Revolution
Returned to Latveria. Overthrew the tyrant King Vladimir. Established a new order where poverty, crime, and hunger were eliminated under my absolute rule.
PRESENT
The Time Platform
Successfully engineered the first stable Time Platform. Currently monitoring multiple timelines to prevent universal collapse and ensure Latverian supremacy.

Global Peace
